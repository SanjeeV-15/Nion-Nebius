Nion — product requirements

A voice-first companion for travelers and anyone on the move. You talk, it talks back, and it's designed for ears, not screens.

Look and feel

  • Modern, sleek, realistic
  • Monotone, between black and white
  • Glass-like, frosted surfaces
  • Not colorful; no big chunky buttons

Behavior and how we'll know it works

Open each area. Every criterion is something you can check by watching or listening.

Voice conversation
  • "Hey Nion" plus a question gets a spoken answer without touching the screen
  • The state icon visibly differs for waiting, listening, thinking and speaking
  • "Hey Nion, let's start a conversation" lets you keep talking without repeating the wake phrase
  • "End the conversation" ends it, and Nion stops listening; close variants work and mishearings are tolerated
  • After an answer, you have 15 seconds to ask a follow-up without saying "Hey Nion"; then Nion goes back to waiting
  • If you start talking while Nion is thinking or speaking, it stops at once and listens to you
Designed for ears, not screens
  • Short spoken answers
  • At most five items, then Nion asks whether you want more, narrower, or deeper
  • Never says to look at a screen, link or list
  • A too-broad question gets one short clarifying question first (e.g. which Nobel field)
  • Rambling is fine: Nion gets the point, and for a long or messy message says back in one sentence what it understood before answering
Questions Nion can answer
  • "What is today's date?" → "Today's date is October 11, Sunday"
  • "Time in Denver?" → correct current time, spoken
  • "Places to eat around me, ranked by price" → up to five with price and cuisine, then a follow-up question
  • Airport to Union Station → a few spoken options
  • General and world knowledge, spoken
Current events and sources
  • Uses multiple sources and says aloud where each point comes from
  • Market questions get a short spoken summary; Nion does not advise what to buy or sell
  • If no source covers it: "I couldn't find any source that mentioned this"
Where am I?
  • If Nion doesn't know your location, it says aloud: allow location services, or just tell me where you are
  • Saying "I'm in Miami" lets it continue the earlier question
  • City, state or a street address all work
A partner with a point of view
  • Describe a small decision (like the cake) and Nion gives an opinion and asks a follow-up
  • Asks your name once by voice the first time you use it, and remembers it
  • Greets you by name at the start of each new conversation, not on every turn
History and transcripts
  • Conversations appear in History with their transcript
  • Open one and copy its text

States and boundaries

First useNeeds microphone access, asked with a short explanation. Location is asked only when a local question needs it, by voice. assumption Nion then asks your name once, by voice.
Not foundNion says it couldn't find a source for the question.
No locationNion asks aloud: allow location, or tell me where you are.
PersistsPast conversations, transcripts and your name stay on the device between sessions.
PrivacyTranscripts belong to the person; nothing is shared.

Building

  • Main screen with the four-state icon
  • Voice in and voice out
  • Conversation started and ended by voice
  • Five-item rule and clarifying questions
  • Date, time, local, directions, general and current-events questions with sources
  • Spoken fallback for missing location
  • Opinions, follow-ups, asks your name once and greets you by name per conversation
  • History with copyable transcripts

Deferred

  • Screen-off and background listening, floating dot
  • Settings for speed, tone and voice
  • Learning from past conversations
  • Your own stocks and portfolio
  • Messages, email, to-dos, reminders, booking
  • Custom catchphrase
  • A tidied-up (Wispr Flow-style) transcript of what you said
  • Watch and other devices

Non-goals

  • No investment advice. Nion summarizes markets only.
  • No lists or links as the answer. It is voice-first.
  • No acting on your accounts in this version.
